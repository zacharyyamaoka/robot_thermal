1 · Tonight's rule (v1): an I²t bucket and a board cut

Per drive, every tick: heat += (I² − I_cont²)·dt above continuous; below it, heat −= min(C / 55 s, I_cont² − I²)·dt, never below 0; C = (I_peak² − I_cont²)·5 s. While the bucket is under C and the board is below 60 °C (re-armed at 55 °C), the drive may go to I_peak; otherwise it clamps to I_cont until the bucket empties. Every number is cited: a datasheet, the boards' own config, or Zach's words (his standing no-artificial-guards rule).

  • Shoulders J1/J2/C1 (JMC 60ASM400 + AD60): I_cont {jmc_ic:.1f} A (JMC's drawing: 14.5 A RMS = 20.5 A amplitude), I_peak {jmc_ip:.1f} A (the boards' cap = the AD60's 18 N·m). 5 s at the cap, refilled in {jmc_refill:.0f} s at rest.
  • Wrists and claw (GIM4305-10): I_cont {gim_ic:.2f} A (rated 1.05 N·m), I_peak {gim_ip:.1f} A (peak 3.82 N·m). 5 s at the peak, refilled in {gim_refill:.0f} s, because at 3.6× continuous the copper's cooling sets the refill. Today's 2.0 A board cap sits below I_cont.
  • Board cut at 60 °C: where moteus starts its own derate (fault 80 − margin 20).
  • The refill is the slower of Zach's minute and the copper's I_cont² cooling. So over any run the I²·t delivered is at most I_cont²·T plus the heat still in the bucket (a greedy controller, 30 min, tests/test_burst.py).
Recommended margins, waiting on Zach (not applied). The datasheet rules fail v2's own pessimistic check: a long hold at I_cont in a 30 °C room, then a full burst.
  • GIM: the 2.06 A hold alone settles the winding at 78 °C (prior) / 103 °C (hot bound), against SteadyWin's only stated temperature, 80 °C. The proposal is I_cont 1.5 A and I_peak 4.2 A, which costs 27 % continuous and 44 % burst torque. It is moot while the wrist boards cap at 2.0 A.
  • JMC: a hold at 20.5 A reaches 130 / 204 °C against IEC 60034-1's 145 °C class-F limit. The proposal is I_cont 17 A, which costs 17 % continuous torque and has no effect at tonight's loads.
The winding parameters behind this are physics priors, so it is evidence, not a measurement; E1 settles it (drives/proposed_margins.py, tests/test_v1_v2.py).

2 · The model (v2)

  • Linear in the state given the current (copper's α·T_w·I² term sits in A), so the observer is an exact linear Kalman filter over [T_w, T_h, T_b, T_a]. It fuses the board reading every moteus gives, plus a resistance-derived winding temperature or a housing thermistor where they exist.
  • Two models. With only the board measured, the winding is not observable, so a lone filter would overclaim. A pessimistic "hot" network (capacities and conductances 2 prior sds low) carries the winding between direct measurements. The protection outputs, winding_hi_c and time_to_limit_s, read it.
  • Fit: MAP least squares in log-parameters with the prior as a penalty, so an unexcited parameter stays at its prior and says so. It is Levenberg-Marquardt in pure Python, checked against scipy/numpy oracles.
  • Board priors are mjbots' own measurement of bare boards (r4: 10.0 K/W, 12.7 J/K; c1: 11.3 K/W, 10.7 J/K). JMC and SteadyWin publish no thermal data, so the winding and housing priors are physics estimates with log sd 0.5 (×/÷1.65).

  • GIM: the winding binds. At 4.2 A (the proposed peak) from 25 °C it takes {gim_ttl_45:.0f} s on the prior and {gim_ttl_45_hot:.0f} s on the hot bound. 7 · Where it stands

Done and proved

  • v1 rule committed with its acceptance test, sent to the rig's owner and adopted. It gates fast arm submits and is replayed on every real run.
  • v2 model, observer and fit pass the hidden-ground-truth test: parameters within ±25 % (core) and ±50 % (weak), held-out winding within the stated bounds, and the bound holds even unfitted. {n_pass} tests pass, plus {n_xfail} findings kept as strict xfails (one board-identifiability ruler, and the datasheet rules failing v2's hot bound); the runtime core imports with no site-packages.
  • Board-node fit from tonight's logs, with spreads and a held-out check that beats "no change" on J1, J2 and C1.

Only modelled, not measured

  • Every winding temperature on the real arm. Until E1 runs, the winding is a datasheet prior. The protection bound is built to sit above it, but that is proven in simulation, not on a motor.

What turns the winding guess into a measurement

  • E1, about 40 min on the rack's hardstop stations: CAN 13 (JMC + AD60) and CAN 1 (GIM). It uses d-axis heating (no torque) and a resistance check every 60 s. Per Zach, it never runs on the pendulum or the arm. The spec is docs/EXPERIMENTS.md; it waits for a bus gap from the W2 goal's pendulum work.
  • E2, a 10 kΩ NTC on J2's end turns, which moteus reads natively. That is a hardware change and Zach's call.
